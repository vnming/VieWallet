Open **Send money**, enter a wallet number or an e-mail and choose **Check**. The app shows a masked name (for example NGUYEN V*** A) so you can confirm it.

## Safety notes

- First transfer to someone: the app reminds you to double-check.
- Large or unusual transfers may be held for a check (up to 72 hours). If not approved, the money returns to your wallet.
- Sent to the wrong person: open a support ticket with the reference.
