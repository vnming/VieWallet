Open **Deposit**, enter an amount (a multiple of 1,000 VND) and continue to SimBank. Choose **Approve** to finish.

## Scenarios by amount

- Ending in 111,000: the bank refuses.
- Ending in 222,000: completes after a few minutes.
- Ending in 777,000: the bank is unavailable.

## Waiting too long?

You can leave the page. We ask the bank again and notify you of the result.
