VieWallet is a portfolio project that simulates an e-wallet. Every balance, transaction and bank is simulated.

## What to know

- Do not enter real personal details, real bank accounts or real identity documents.
- **SimBank** is a simulated bank page: you approve or decline yourself.
- Amounts ending with certain digits trigger simulated scenarios (for example a failure or a delay).

## Why PIN, KYC and limits then?

So the experience matches a real wallet: the safety steps are complete, only the money is fictional.
