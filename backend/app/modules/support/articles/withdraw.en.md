Withdrawals need **identity verification (KYC)**. Add a fictional bank account under **Wallet → Bank accounts**; a new account can be used after 1 hour.

## Fee and held money

The fee is 1,100 VND. When you confirm, the amount plus the fee is held until the bank answers. If it fails, everything goes back to your wallet.
