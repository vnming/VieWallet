## Steps

- Sign up with an e-mail, a password and your name.
- Enter the 6-digit code sent to your inbox (Mailpit in development).
- Set a 6-digit **transaction PIN**. The PIN confirms every payment that takes money out.

## Keep the PIN safe

Never share it, not even with support staff. After 5 wrong attempts it is locked for 30 minutes.
