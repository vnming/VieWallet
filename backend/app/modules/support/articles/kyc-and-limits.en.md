New accounts are **tier 0**: at most 2,000,000 VND per transfer, a 10,000,000 VND balance cap, no withdrawals.

## Reach tier 1

Open **Verify**, enter fictional details and upload sample images (never real documents). Staff review it. Tier 1 lasts 2 years.

## See your limits

**Wallet → Limits** shows what is used and left today and this month.
