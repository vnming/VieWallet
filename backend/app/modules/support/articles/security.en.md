## Lost your phone?

Go to **Security → Freeze wallet**. The wallet stops sending and receiving at once; unfreeze with your PIN.

## Sessions

See signed-in devices and sign them out remotely under **Security**.

## Forgot the PIN

Reset it with your password and an e-mailed code. Large payments are blocked for 24 hours afterwards to protect you.
